The brand's decorative mark — a violet cap with mint spore lights and one amber lamp.

```jsx
<GlowingMushroom size="lg" style={{ position: 'absolute', left: -20, bottom: -20,
  opacity: 0.5, animation: 'kn-float 8s ease-in-out infinite' }} />
```

Always absolutely positioned, always 40–80% opacity, always drifting on `kn-float` or `kn-float-reverse`. Two or three per section at most, and let them bleed past the panel edge.
