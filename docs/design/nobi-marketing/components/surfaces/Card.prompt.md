The surface primitive — every box on the site is one of its four tones.

```jsx
<Card tone="lifted" padding={32}>…</Card>
```

`lifted` carries the top-to-bottom violet wash and a 16px radius (feature cards); `default` is the flat 12px infrastructure tile; `muted` is the same tile at 75% for unshipped things; `panel` is the solid #13111C app window with a violet bloom.
